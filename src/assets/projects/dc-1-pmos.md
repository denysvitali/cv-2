Port of Linux (postmarketOS) to the [Daylight Computer DC-1](https://daylightcomputer.com), built with the help of AI agents ([announcement](https://x.com/DenysVitali/status/2087645896938865097)). The port was [well received by Daylight](https://x.com/daylightco/status/2094406300184805700) and complimented by their CEO, and a growing number of DC-1 owners now run Linux on their device thanks to it.
